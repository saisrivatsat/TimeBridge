# Code of Conduct

TimeBridge Link is a welcoming project. Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss
ideas rather than people, and make room for contributors with different levels
of experience and different ways of communicating.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, or publishing another person's private
information are not accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that
violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict access.

To report a concern privately, use the security contact method described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as promptly and discreetly
as practical.
